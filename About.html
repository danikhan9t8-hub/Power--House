About PowerHub

Your trusted partner for premium electrical, hardware, solar, plumbing and home improvement products. Quality you can rely on since 2015.

Our Story

PowerHub was founded in 2015 with a simple mission: to provide homeowners, electricians, and contractors with the highest quality electrical and hardware products at competitive prices.

What started as a small local store in New York has grown into a trusted online destination for thousands of customers across the country. We believe that every project, big or small, deserves the best materials and tools.

Today, we stock over 1,500 products across 8 categories, partnering with industry-leading brands to ensure our customers get nothing but the best.
